Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with Tech Nova.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Tech Nova ("we", "us", "our") is a technology, security and infrastructure company based in St. Michael, Barbados. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it. We keep it plain and short on purpose.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you fill in our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the type of enquiry you select, and your message. We do not ask for, and you should never send us, sensitive information such as passwords or payment card numbers through this website.

Like most websites, we may also collect basic, anonymous usage data (such as pages visited and general location) through analytics tools to understand how the site is used and improve it.

How we use it

We use your contact details for one reason: to respond to your enquiry and provide the products, services or quotes you ask about. Submissions are sent to our business email and recorded in our own private records so we can follow up. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

Who can see it

Your information is handled by Tech Nova staff and by the trusted service providers that run our email, analytics and form processing (for example Google). These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to measure traffic anonymously. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Keeping your information

We keep enquiry information only as long as we need it to serve you and to keep proper business records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or want us to update or remove your details? Email Juwanprescod@Technovabb.com or call (246) 230-8303.

This policy is provided for general information and reflects how the website works. For a formal legal policy, we recommend a quick review by a qualified professional.
