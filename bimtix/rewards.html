How it works

  1. Buy tickets

    Earn 1 point for every BBD $1 you spend on tickets.

  2. Collect points

    Points land in your account as soon as you pay.

  3. Use them

    1,000 points = BBD $10 off at checkout. Just tick “Use points”.

  4. Level up

    Spend more over the year to unlock Gold and Platinum perks.

Questions

Do I need an account to earn points?

Yes. Guests can still buy tickets, but points and saved events need a free account.

Can I use points on any event?

Most events. A promoter may leave a few special events out — the checkout will always tell you.

Do points expire?

To be decided. A common choice is 12 months after your last purchase.

What happens to points if I get a refund?

Points from refunded tickets are taken back, and points you used are returned.
