Work through it together

Six labs on what we do on every job. Do the steps together on the call: you do them on your own laptop and phone while Juwan talks you through. Then the practice questions, then send the lab to Juwan.

How it works

  1. Your ticks and answers save on this phone or laptop, so use the same device each time.
  2. Type answers into the boxes. They save when you tap or click away.
  3. When you finish a lab, press Send to Juwan at the bottom. WhatsApp opens with your ticks and answers ready to send.
  4. Juwan checks your work and signs the lab off on your training record.
Bench rule: training gear is never plugged into the office network or a client's network. A lab router hands out addresses and can knock a whole office offline (call-out 9 in Fault finding shows how).
