How the numbers are worked out

These are sample rates so you can see the shape of the business. They’re not decided yet.

  • Buyers pay the ticket price plus a 5% BimTix service fee.
  • Promoter payout = ticket sales − refunds, sent 3 days after the event once you approve it.
  • Card processing is estimated at 3.5% of what buyers pay, until we choose the Barbados payment provider.
  • Rewards points used by buyers are paid for by BimTix, not the promoter: the “BimTix fees” column is after points discounts.
  • BimTix keeps the service fees, minus points discounts and card processing.
  • Every payout needs both owners to approve it, then one of you marks it sent with the bank reference.
